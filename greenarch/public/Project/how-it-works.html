Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the whole process take?

Most projects take 4-12 weeks from booking to transformation, depending on scope. We'll provide a clear timeline during the inspection phase based on your specific project.

What if I have a limited budget?

We work at all budget levels. During inspection, our experts will propose solutions scaled to your budget, from minimal setup to comprehensive transformation.

Can I change my plan mid-project?

Yes. We're flexible and can adapt plans as needed. Any changes will be communicated with updated costs and timelines upfront.

Do you provide maintenance after completion?

We offer optional maintenance packages to keep your green space thriving. Many clients choose ongoing quarterly visits or as-needed support.

What services do you offer?

We specialize in gardening setup and maintenance, solar installation coordination, and green vendor sourcing. Learn more on our Services page.

How do I get started?

Simply reach out through the Contact page and share details about your space. We'll contact you within 24 hours to schedule your consultation.
