I'm GreenArch. The team rethinking urban green living.

A platform built for better spaces, cleaner air, and everyday wellbeing.

GreenArch started with one clear frustration: city homes had unused corners, dead balconies, and empty terraces that could become living ecosystems.

"You do not need a huge garden to live greener. You need the right system and support."

Whether you are a first-time homeowner or part of a residential society, GreenArch helps you plan, build, and maintain practical green spaces with confidence.

My Story

From unused corners to thriving green spaces in every home.

We saw how urban life disconnects people from nature. Apartments keep growing upward, while access to fresh, living green spaces keeps shrinking.

Most people want greenery, but they are unsure where to begin, what plants suit their space, and how to maintain everything long-term.

"What if every balcony, terrace, and indoor corner could be transformed into a simple green ecosystem?"

That question became GreenArch. Not just as a service, but as a long-term movement toward healthier urban living.
